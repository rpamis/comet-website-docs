---
title: "Split a large PRD"
description: "Break a large requirement, roadmap, or product plan into independently designable, deliverable, verifiable, and archivable changes."
---

## What it solves

Large PRDs often contain multiple independent changes. Comet can split them into smaller OpenSpec changes so each has a clear proposal, design, build, verify, and archive path.

## When splitting is triggered

Splitting is useful when the request contains unrelated domains, multiple user journeys, separate rollout risks, independently deliverable work, or scope that would make one design too broad.

## Splitting flow

Comet identifies candidate changes, asks for your decision, and then creates bounded changes instead of one oversized workflow.

## Your choices

You can usually choose to split now, keep one workflow, or defer part of the scope.

## How to proceed after splitting

Work through changes one at a time unless you intentionally parallelize them. Archive completed changes before mixing unrelated work.

## Split and presets

Hotfix and tweak work should stay small. If a preset expands into multi-change scope, upgrade to the full workflow and split deliberately.
