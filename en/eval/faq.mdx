---
title: "Eval FAQ"
description: "Common questions about comet eval, collect mode, pass metrics, task setup, and publish evidence."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Should I run --collect first?">
    Yes. It catches discovery and configuration issues without spending model tokens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between pass@k and pass^k?">
    pass@k means at least one attempt passed. pass^k means every attempt passed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can eval failures come from the harness?">
    Yes. Check failure attribution before changing the Skill.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do generated Skills need a custom task?">
    Usually no. `/comet-any` generated packages include `comet/eval.yaml`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
