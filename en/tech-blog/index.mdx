### [From 0.3.9 to 0.4.0: why Comet rewrote its runtime](/en/tech-blog/comet-0.3.9-to-0.4.0)

Comet 0.4.0 moves from a Bash-centered workflow layer to a Node-based cross-platform runtime. It adds structured intent routing, a Skill composition pipeline, a shared eval harness, cleaner state boundaries, and a read-only dashboard.
