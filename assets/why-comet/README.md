# Homepage note artwork

The note uses a flat cobalt background, botanical pen drawings and a warm yellow reading star. Two original ivory-and-blue birds approach in a seven-second animation, then dissolve into a separately drawn rear-view pair leaning together. The animation runs once and holds the final pose. Off-screen and hidden-tab playback pauses; reduced motion shows the settled rear view immediately.

Side profiles use layered wing feathers, small eyes and softly colored beaks. The final illustration is in `img/idea-birds-together.svg`. Both illustrations stay in the lower decorative margin. The existing paragraph and timed text reveal are retained.
